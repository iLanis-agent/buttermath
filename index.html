Butter math

A carton of cream is a promise: shake it long enough and it splits into butter and buttermilk. How much of each, how long the churn takes, and how much salt and rinse water the finished block wants - three small calculators, exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Cream to butter - a carton of cream becomes butter and buttermilk at the labeled yield.
  • Churn time - method and cream weight become honest minutes, from jar shake to processor.
  • Salt and wash - the block becomes salt to weigh and rinse water to ready.

Labeled norms

Butter yield 45% of cream weight, buttermilk is the rest. Churn minutes scale from a 500 g base: jar shake 20 min, hand mixer 10, stand mixer 6, food processor 4. Salted butter 1.5% salt. Three rinse rounds, 1 mL of cold water per gram of butter per round. These are common kitchen norms, not a recipe - your cream, your churn and your taste win.
